03How to attribute it

One line, and — where you are citing a specific figure — a link to the page that figure came from, so a reader can verify it against the filing the way you did.

General attribution Source: The Grants Ledger, Curry Studio — https://jcurry44.github.io/grants-ledgers/
Citing a specific foundation figure Source: The Grants Ledger, Curry Studio, from IRS Form 990-PF — [link to that foundation’s ledger page], retrieved [date].

Include the retrieval date when the figure matters. The collection is refreshed on a schedule and a foundation’s record can gain a year, or have a year withheld, between one reading and the next; the date is what lets a reader reconcile your figure with what they see.

If you are describing where a figure comes from, the accurate phrasing is that it is reconciled to a total the foundation filed with the IRS. Please do not describe it as audited, certified, verified by the IRS, or endorsed by the foundation. None of those is true.

04Accuracy, and the limits of it

This is a documented reconciliation of public filings. It is not an audit.

Every year published here has been checked so that the individual grants listed add up, to the dollar, to a grant total the foundation itself filed with the IRS. A year that does not tie is withheld rather than adjusted, and every withheld year in the collection is named on the exceptions page. That is a real and unusually strong guarantee, and it is a narrower one than it may first appear. It is worth being exact about what it does and does not cover.

What the reconciliation does establish

That the rows shown for a year are complete and correctly totalled with respect to the foundation’s own filed figure — that nothing has been dropped, duplicated, mistyped in the amount column, or invented.

What it cannot establish

That the filing itself is right. If a foundation filed a figure incorrectly, this site reproduces the incorrect figure, because the filing is the source of record. Several such internal contradictions have been found and are published rather than smoothed over; the findings page collects them.

That a recipient name identifies the organisation you think it does. Form 990-PF does not ask for the recipient’s EIN, so no grant row here carries one as filed. Recipients matched to an IRS-registered organisation are identified by government record; the majority are grouped by normalised name and may split one organisation in two or hold two same-named organisations as one. The data limits give the exact proportions.

That the collection is complete. It is the top of the field by assets — 1,904 of the largest 2,000 U.S. private foundations plus a 29-foundation Western New York slate — and nothing below that floor. It is not a census of American philanthropy, and an absence from it is not evidence that nobody funded something.

That a state total is a state’s giving. 16.22% of rows carry no recipient state in the filing. Place-based figures are floors, not shares.

05Reporting a suspected error

This matters more than anything else on this page. A product whose entire claim is accuracy has to make being told it is wrong easy, and has to act when it is told.

  1. Email jjcurry027@gmail.com with Correction in the subject line.
  2. Include the page URL, the foundation, and the tax year.
  3. Say which figure you believe is wrong, and what you believe it should be.
  4. Where you can, name the source — ideally the filing itself, or the page of the attachment.

What then happens. The report is checked against the original filing rather than against the page. If the page is wrong, it is corrected. If the figure cannot be proven from the filing, the year is withheld and the reason is published on the exceptions page — a number that cannot be sourced does not stay up in a softened form. Either way the change is recorded in the update history.

If you are the foundation. A report about your own foundation’s page goes to the front of the queue. If your filed record contains something you believe is wrong, note that this site reproduces filings rather than correcting them — the durable fix is an amended return, which the monthly refresh will pick up. In the meantime the disagreement can be disclosed on the page.

Realistic expectations for response times, and what support does and does not exist, are set out on the about page.
