        The referee

        Every filed year is shown, and a year that does not reconcile shows its difference

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is shown with its difference, never estimated away. 3 of the five available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        How a year is counted

        This foundation’s tax year ends August 31, so year labels here always follow the filing, never the calendar. Every year reconciles to the filed total to the dollar.

        Four of the five years here itemize fully — 1,009 named grants, mostly to university science programs. The earliest year instead reports one line, “SEE GRANT DETAILS ATTACHED,” for its whole $30,639,349, and the IRS has not posted a scanned copy of that return, so its recipients are not publicly recoverable today. The roll-up row is kept as filed — it reconciles to the filed total to the dollar — and the year will be itemized if the scan appears.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
