Evidence and limitations

Methodology

What the ledger measures, how it reconciles, and where interpretation must remain careful.

A document that referees itself

Grant rows come from the grants-paid schedule in each public IRS Form 990-PF XML filing. For current filings this is Part XIV; older forms labeled the comparable schedule Part XV. The sum of published grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d), charitable disbursements. If a year does not reconcile, it is held back.

Coverage

This release contains the five Oishei tax years available in IRS TEOS e-file XML: 2020 through 2024. It does not imply that the foundation made no earlier grants; earlier history would require a separately verified PDF workflow.

Canonical recipients

Confirmed filing-name variations are consolidated for relationship analysis while every original name remains attached to its grant row. The current crosswalk is deliberately conservative and should be reviewed when new filings arrive.

Purpose signals

Overview purpose groupings are transparent keyword signals derived from the filed purpose text. They are analytical aids, not official program classifications assigned by the foundation.

Location

Locations shown are recipient mailing addresses from the filing. They are not service areas and should not be interpreted as the geography of grant impact.
