        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. 4 of 5 available years pass — 1 withheld; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        About the roll-up lines

        One year carries a one-dollar difference, and it is the filing's own. In TY2021 the grants register prints 104 rows summing to $9,122,648, while the register's own printed total, Part XV line 3a and Part I line 25(d) all three print $9,122,647. The rows were read twice over — a three-resolution reading, then a full eye-read of the amount column against magnified crops of every page, then an independent hand-sum — and every row matches the paper. The cause is the register printing whole dollars over a cents-true book: about eight rows carry fractions, which predicts a rounding difference near a dollar, and a dollar is what appears. The year’s rows are kept exactly as filed and nothing has been adjusted to force a tie; because they do not reconcile to the filed total, TY2021 is withheld from this ledger and named on the exceptions page.

        Part of this foundation's grantmaking is reported as roll-up lines pointing to filed attachments rather than itemized recipients — $15,617,598 of the $73,045,696 here (21.4%). Those lines are printed exactly as filed and reconcile to the filed totals like everything else; the machine-readable filings do not itemize them. Where the IRS's rendered copies carry the underlying lists, this ledger recovers them over time.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
