        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. All five available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        Four years read from the scans

        All four available years were recovered from the IRS's scanned copies — 964 grants reconciling to the cent against each attachment's own printed total, and through it to the filed line. Two of those registers print their totals twice, once with and once without program-related investments; the filed grants line equals the figure WITHOUT them, so the two investment rows (Lift Up Enterprises Holdings and the Chicago Trend Corporation) are excluded here, exactly as the foundation's own arithmetic excludes them. Seventeen amounts of a million dollars or more were invisible to the reading software at every resolution and were read from the page images by hand. Two filing defects are worth naming: 2020's grants page reaches the public copy as a title band with no table beneath it, and 2022's grants-total cell is simply left blank — in both years the filed line and the register's own total carry the proof instead. The recurring names are Harvard, the Carnegie Endowment, Chicago Public Media and the Greater Auburn Gresham Development Corporation. The most recent year — $120.5 million, larger than these four combined — has not been posted as a scan yet.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
