        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. All five available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        About these rows

        Every row below is a filed row — the schedule as the foundation reported it, summing to the filed total to the dollar.

        1 of the 1,437 rows across these years are the foundation’s own aggregate lines rather than named recipients — “SEE YOU AT THE TOP” — totaling $5,000, 0.0% of the dollars here. They are printed exactly as filed; they reconcile along with everything else.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
