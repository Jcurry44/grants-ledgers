        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. All five available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        Four years read from sideways registers

        All four available years were recovered from the IRS's scanned copies — 294 grants, each year reconciling to the cents against the register's own printed total and through it to the filed line. The registers are landscape sheets stored sideways in the filings and had to be turned upright before anything could be read; forty-one amounts of a million dollars or more were invisible to the reading software at every resolution and were read from the page images by hand, each one checked against the width of its own printed digits. Four addresses in 2022 are blank on the page itself and are left blank here rather than invented, and 2020's grants page reaches the public copy as a header strip with no table beneath it — that year's proof rests on the filed line and the register's own total.

        The book is steadily Chicago: the University of Chicago receives $4 million, $6 million, $8 million and $10 million across the four years, alongside the Greater Chicago Food Depository, Lurie Children's, and the University of Illinois Foundation. Every row's stated purpose is general operating support. The most recent year's scanned return is not posted yet.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
