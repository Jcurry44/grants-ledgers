        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. All five available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        How two years were read, and what one year withholds

        2022's and 2023's recipient lists were recovered from the IRS's scanned copies — sideways-printed registers of cash grants and Amazon-stock gifts valued at fair market value, self-paginated by the filer and reconciled section by section: each year's cash and stock subtotals prove to the cent against the attachment's own printed figures, and the years tie the filed line (the attachment carries cents the form rounds). The stock register also prints a book-value column this ledger excludes; in 2023 that column's own cells sum $1,533,193 more than the filer's printed book total — an arithmetic error inside the filing, noted here and left exactly as filed, touching none of the reconciled money. The largest recovered rows are Amazon-share gifts: $15.1 million to Columbia University, $9.9 million to the Children's Museum of Manhattan, and repeated support for Narrative 4, the Aspen Institute, and Common Sense Media.

        2021 is withheld: its stub points to an “Attachment C” that is absent from every public copy of that return — all 36 pages inventoried, the appended attachments end at B — so its $77.6 million stays as the filed roll-up line. 2020 and 2024 ($74.3 million and $349.9 million) await their scanned copies.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
