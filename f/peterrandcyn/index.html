        The referee

        Halt-on-fail: a year publishes only when it reconciles exactly

        Grant rows come from the Form 990-PF grants-paid schedule — Part XIV in current filings, formerly Part XV. For every tax year, the sum of the grant rows must equal Part I, line 25, column (d) — charitable disbursements — to the dollar. A year that does not tie is withheld, never estimated. All six available years pass; the same checks re-run in your browser’s console on every load.

        How two years were read from the scans

        This foundation’s tax year ends June 30, so year labels here always follow the filing, never the calendar. Every year reconciles to the filed total to the dollar.

        The filed return for 2022, 2023 carries its grant list only as an attachment, so the 501 rows on this page for those years were read from the IRS's posted scan with local OCR at two resolutions, and an amount was kept only when both readings agreed. 22 cells the two readings disagreed on were re-read at four further resolutions and kept only where two of them agreed. 2 cells the engine could not read at any resolution were read by eye from the page image. Every recovered year sums to the filed line 25(d) to the dollar, with cents apportioned at no more than one dollar on any row; a year that does not is not shown as rows. Years shown as a single not-itemized row (2019, 2020, 2021, 2024) have no readable schedule in any public copy of the filing and carry their filed totals unbroken.

          The purpose map

          “Where a granted dollar goes” assigns each grant to one category by the words of the filing itself — first the filed purpose, then (only when the purpose names no field) the recipient’s filed name. Every rule is published below; anything no rule matches is left unassigned rather than guessed. The filed purpose text on every ledger row remains the record.

          Limitations

          Recipient location is the mailing address on the filing, not a service area. Display casing is editorial; hover any name for the as-filed text. Amounts are as filed; no inflation adjustment. Nothing here is an audit, attestation, or opinion — it is a documented reconciliation of the foundation’s own public filings, with the receipts attached.
