Research decision

Hold for verification
Why investigate
The filed purpose names resident leadership, environmental and health justice, and Richmond. That makes the record relevant to the example research question.
Application route
Unknown. Current funder guidance has not been verified for this example.
Current eligibility
Unknown. A past award does not establish whether a new applicant can apply.
Next step
Check current program priorities, geographic restrictions, and whether unsolicited inquiries are accepted. Record the source and date checked.

What this evidence does not support

Keep this candidate off a ready-to-apply list until the route is verified. Exclude the single $45,000 award as a “typical grant” benchmark; one record cannot establish a suitable ask range.

The award details are taken from this site’s historical ledger. The assessment illustrates the brief’s format, not a current funding recommendation. Urban Tilth is a historical recipient, not a pilot client.
